See your impact

Morphic counts how often people use the computer's built-in accessibility features. You can keep the MorphicBar hidden for a while first, then compare use before and after it appears.

  • Useful for showing Morphic's value in reports.
  • Counts are recorded per computer, then added up in your reports.
